The drive from Reno

Reno to Mammoth down the 395 — hot springs, a ghost town, tufa towers, and the June Lake Loop. Roughly four hours of driving; budget the day.

Monday · Aug 3

Two legs, zero hurry

The 395 south from Reno is one of the great American drives — high desert, hot springs, a ghost town, and a string of alpine lakes. Split it into two legs and stop wherever calls to you.

Leg 1Reno → Mono Lake · ~2.5 hr driving

Down the 395

Out of the neon and into the sage. The Sierra rises on your right the whole way south — with a hot spring and a ghost town if you have the time.

Mono Lake · South Tufa

An inland sea older than the ice ages, rimmed with limestone towers that rise straight out of the water. A short, flat boardwalk wander — and nothing else on the drive looks remotely like it.

Recommended · adds ~10 min

June Lake Loop

Sixteen miles off the 395 that feel like Switzerland — Grant, Silver, Gull, and June strung beneath 10,000-foot walls, with a swimmable beach before the final twenty minutes home.
